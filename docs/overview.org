#+title: Gigawatt Galaxy: Design

An incremental simulation game where players manage a celestial system. By
launching objects into orbit around a central star, players generate energy
based on orbital mechanics. The game aims to blend the chaotic beauty of n-body
physics with the satisfying progression of automation and eventual prestige.

** Gameplay loops & mechanics

The primary loop involves spending energy to launch objects, maintaining stable
orbits to maximize energy generation, and managing collisions to evolve
celestial bodies.

*** Energy generation

- The central star provides a baseline energy emission based on its mass and
  radius.
- Orbiting objects generate energy scaled by their size, kinetic energy,
  proximity to the star, and longevity.
- Shattered objects drop energy fragments that must be manually collected via
  hover.
- Out-of-bounds objects provide a minor balance-recovery energy payout.

*** Launch & Collision mechanics

Players use a slingshot mechanic to launch payloads. Launches will cost energy
based on type of the object and the slingshot distance
Collisions result in three outcomes based on size and velocity:
- High velocity, similar size. Results in energy bursts and both bodies get
  destroyed.
- Low velocity, similar size. Objects merge into one and evolve into higher-tier
  bodies (e.g., planets to gas giants).
- Collision with stars will increase the mass and radius of the star and
 destroy the object colliding with it.

**  Progression and Economy

*** Phase 1: Manual Discovery

Initial gameplay focuses on discovery. New object types encountered through
physics interactions are added to the player's permanent library. Examples
include Standard Comets, Heavier Planets, Antimatter Comets, and Dense Iron
Cores.

*** Phase 2: Automation & Upgrades

- Players unlock tools to automate the system like adding emitters that
  Periodically launch pecific objects for a set duration.
- Upgrade Tree. Players can spend energy to modify properties like the
  following:
    - the Gravitational Constant. This will increase the velocty of objects.
    - the Central Star's properties (Mass/Radius).
    - emitter properties like speed, cost etc.
    - slingshot properties like preview duration, cost etc.
    - properties of individual objects

*** Phase 3: Prestige (Supernova)

When the star becomes too massive for orbits to escape, the system collapses
into a Black Hole or Supernova. This acts as a soft reset, providing premium
currency and permanent multipliers for the next run.
